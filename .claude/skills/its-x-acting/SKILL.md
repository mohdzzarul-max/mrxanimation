# Character acting and expressions

## Map every emotion to the approved sheets (never invent new faces)
| Beat | Expression panel (D) | Eyes (E) | Mouth (F) | Body |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Concentrating | FOKUS | FOCUS (INTENS) or HALF-LID | IDLE, lips pressed | lean in, shoulders forward, hands precise |
| Curious / sceptical | SKEPTIKAL | HALF-LID, ONE brow up | I or GRIT corner | head cocks 10-15°, weight back slightly |
| Thinking | FIKIR | OPEN, eyes up/side | IDLE | hand to chin (pose T10/T15) |
| Surprised | TERKEJUT | WIDE (SURPRISE) | O → SHOUT | half-step back, shoulders up, open hands (pose T16/T17) |
| Worried | RISAU | OPEN, brows tilted up inside | E | shoulders in, a hand near the chest |
| Confident | YAKIN / SENYUM | OPEN | SMILE | chest up, chin up |
| Angry | MARAH | FOCUS | GRIT / SHOUT | lean forward, fists |
| Exhausted | LETIH | HALF-LID | E / open | slumped, a hand on the head (T19) |
Mr X: use sheet G (masked) - NORMAL, SKEPTIKAL, MARAH, BENGANG, TERKEJUT, FOKUS - eye shapes only, no mouth.

## Acting rules
- **Eyes lead**: eyes move first, the head follows 2-4 frames later, the body last. A look change without the head = thought; with the head = decision.
- **Blinks**: 1 frame half → 1-2 frames closed → 1 frame half. Blink on head turns and on changes of thought. Not on a fixed rhythm; roughly every 2-4 s when idle.
- **Brows** carry thought: asymmetric (one up) = doubt; both up = surprise; inner ends down = focus or anger. Brows move 2 frames BEFORE the mouth.
- **Head turns** pass through the B-panel angles in order (front → 3/4 → side), with a slight down-dip in the middle of the turn (an arc), never a flat swivel.
- **Mouth**: in dialogue use the F set (IDLE A E I O U SMILE GRIT SHOUT). Hold mouth shapes for at least 2 frames. Accent the stressed syllable.
- **Reactions** follow: notice (eyes) → process (brows or blink) → react (body take). Give each a separate beat; do not merge them into one pose.
- **Hands** act too: precise fingertips for lab work (G: GRAB, HOLD), open spread for surprise (OPEN), a fist for resolve.

## Reaction recipe: "strange perfume formula" (scientist)
1. Focus: FOKUS + FOCUS eyes, pipette held with GRAB.
2. Notice: eyes flick to the flask (2 fr), head follows (4 fr later).
3. Doubt: blink, then one brow up (SKEPTIKAL), head cock.
4. Anticipation: small squash down, breath in.
5. Take: TERKEJUT + WIDE eyes + O mouth, half-step back, open hand up (T16/T17).
6. Recover: settle over 6-10 frames on twos; brows lower slightly, hair and coat overshoot then settle.

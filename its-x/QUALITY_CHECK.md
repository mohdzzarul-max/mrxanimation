# Quality check - honest disclosure

## What this is
- A **2D cut-out / rig animation drawn in code** (HTML canvas vector shapes). It is **not traditional hand-drawn, frame-by-frame animation.**
- No bitmap of the character is used. Every part is a separate drawn piece with its own animation:
  - head (angle, pull-back), hair mass, the curved tuft (spring follow-through)
  - two grey eyes (open, half-lid, blink, wide; look direction)
  - two eyebrows, animated separately (furrow, one-brow raise)
  - mouth (pursed, skew, open)
  - torso lean and shoulder lift
  - two arms on 2-bone IK (shoulder, elbow, wrist)
  - three drawn hand poses (pipette grip with squeezing thumb, relaxed, half-open, open). Every hand pose has 4 fingers and a thumb.
  - coat panels with sway, black wristbands
- The **background is your approved `03_ENVIRONMENT/LAB_BACKGROUND_REFERENCE.png`**, used as a painted background plate with a slight blur.
- The flask and the test-tube rack are re-drawn or masked over it so the flask can glow and the rack sits in front of him. The rain in the window is animated.
- Old animatic videos were **not** reused.

## Known gaps vs. the approved sheets
- The face is a **simplified match**, not a 1:1 copy of the sheet's anime-style rendering. The jaw, nose and eye shapes are close, but the line quality is cleaner and simpler. A real hand-drawn pass, or traced drawings from the sheet, would match it better.
- Hair is a simplified spike mass. The sheet's hair has more layered locks.
- The camera is a medium-wide shot, so the face is about 240 px tall. A closer shot would read the acting better.
- Arms are smooth capsules, with no sleeve folds.

## Verdict
This is a playable acting test with readable face, eyes, mouth, arms, hands, torso, hair and coat motion, synced to measured music timing. It is honest cut-out animation. If the goal is hand-drawn quality, the next step is drawn key poses (8-12 drawings) traced from the approved sheets, using this timing as the blueprint.
